## Brand Overview

**Namecheap** is a premium organic beauty and health product marketplace dedicated to bringing sustainable, eco-friendly products to conscious consumers. We promote a profit-sharing community model where customers benefit from bulk discounts, referral commissions, and shared growth.

**Tagline:** "Bringing organic products and community benefits together"

**Mission:** To make premium organic products accessible while building a community where everyone wins.

---

## Brand Values

- **Organic & Pure:** 100% authentic, eco-friendly products
- **Community-First:** Profit-sharing, transparency, and mutual growth
- **Trustworthy:** 10,000+ satisfied customers, secure payments
- **Sustainable:** Eco-friendly practices from sourcing to delivery
- **Accessible:** Bulk discounts, referral programs, cash on delivery options

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo: Shield (Full Version)
- **File:** `namecheaplogo.png`
- **Use Case:** Main brand identifier for favicons, app icons, footer branding
- **Minimum Size:** 64x64 pixels
- **Background:** Transparent (must always use transparent background)
- **Colors:** 
  - Orange section (left): Represents warmth and organic nature
  - Cyan/Blue section (right): Represents trust and stability
  - Purple wing accent: Symbolizes growth and transformation
  - Golden circle with %: Represents discounts and profit-sharing

### Extended Logo: Banner/Poster (Horizontal Format)
- **File:** `logo-banner-xl.png`
- **Use Case:** Header branding, promotional materials, social media banners
- **Minimum Size:** 400 pixels width
- **Background:** Transparent (must always use transparent background)
- **Layout:** Shield logo on left with "NAMECHEAP" text and "EXTRA DISCOUNT" tagline on right
- **Aspect Ratio:** Wide horizontal format (approximately 4:1)

### Logo Variations

#### Clear Space (Minimum Spacing)
- Leave minimum 20% of logo height as clear space on all sides
- Do not place logo closer to edges or other design elements

#### Size Guidelines
- **Large Displays:** 256x256px and above (website header, banners)
- **Medium Displays:** 128x128px (app icons, social media)
- **Small Displays:** 64x64px and below (browser tabs, favicons)
- **Never scale below:** 32x32px (loses legibility)

#### Do's and Don'ts
✅ **DO:**
- Use on transparent backgrounds
- Maintain original proportions
- Apply consistent branding across all platforms
- Use centered or left-aligned positioning

❌ **DON'T:**
- Add drop shadows, outlines, or effects
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Change colors or opacity
- Place on complex/busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
- Use on white or opaque backgrounds

## Typography

### Font Family
**Primary Font:** Geist (Modern, Clean, Sans-Serif)
- **License:** Open Source
- **Use:** All primary headings, body text, UI elements

**Fallback:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif

### Font Hierarchy

| Element | Size | Weight | Color | Usage |
|---------|------|--------|-------|-------|
| **H1 (Page Title)** | 32-48px | Bold (700) | Navy (#1A2D4D) | Main page headings |
| **H2 (Section Title)** | 24-32px | Bold (700) | Navy (#1A2D4D) | Section headers |
| **H3 (Subsection)** | 18-24px | Semibold (600) | Navy (#1A2D4D) | Card titles, subheadings |
| **Body Text** | 14-16px | Regular (400) | Navy (#1A2D4D) | Content, descriptions |
| **Small Text** | 12-14px | Regular (400) | Muted Gray | Captions, metadata |
| **Button Text** | 14-16px | Semibold (600) | White/Navy | CTA, navigation |
| **Tagline** | 14-18px | Regular (400) | Sky Blue (#4DB8D8) | "Extra Discount" subtext |

### Typography Rules

- **Line Spacing:** 1.5 for body text, 1.2 for headings
- **Letter Spacing:** 0.5px for headings, normal for body
- **Text Alignment:** Left-aligned for body, centered for headings/CTAs
- **Emphasis:** Use bold or color change, not italics

## Photography & Imagery

### Product Photography

**Style:** Natural lighting, high-quality, lifestyle-oriented
- **Background:** Clean, minimal white or gradient backgrounds for product shots
- **Angle:** 45-degree angles for depth, straight-on for detail shots
- **Lighting:** Soft, diffused lighting to highlight product details
- **Color Accuracy:** Ensure true-to-life color representation

**Image Specifications:**
- **Minimum Resolution:** 1200x1200px for product images
- **Format:** WebP or optimized PNG/JPG
- **Aspect Ratios:** 1:1 (square), 16:9 (banners), 4:3 (featured)

### Brand Imagery Themes

1. **Organic Elements:** Fresh leaves, natural textures, botanical visuals
2. **Community:** People collaborating, group benefits, shared growth visuals
3. **Quality:** Close-ups of products, quality indicators, certifications
4. **Sustainability:** Eco-friendly materials, green practices, natural packaging

### Image Treatment

- Apply consistent filters for cohesive look (slight warm tone + slight saturation boost)
- Use gradients overlays for text overlay on images (see Toast Gradient section)
- Ensure adequate contrast between images and text overlays
- Avoid heavy filters that obscure product details

## Voice & Tone

### Brand Voice Characteristics

- **Friendly & Approachable:** Conversational, not corporate
- **Trustworthy & Transparent:** Clear communication, honest about products and pricing
- **Community-Focused:** Emphasize "we" and "together" messaging
- **Empowering:** Highlight customer benefits and agency
- **Authentic:** Genuine care for customers and sustainability

### Tone Variations

**Promotional Copy:**
> "Join our community where everyone wins - better prices for you, sustainable growth for us, and shared profits that benefit our entire network of members."

**Product Descriptions:**
> "Carefully sourced organic [product]. [Key benefit]. Trusted by 10,000+ satisfied customers."

**Error Messages:**
> "Something went wrong. We're here to help! Please try again or contact our support team."

**Success Messages:**
> "Amazing! [Action] was successful. You're all set to enjoy [benefit]."

## Design Elements

### Badge & Label Styles

#### Discount Badges
- **Background:** Gradient (Yellow → Orange)
- **Text:** "X% OFF" or "Rs. X OFF"
- **Color:** White text
- **Padding:** 4-8px
- **Border Radius:** 4-6px
- **Font Size:** 12-14px semibold

#### Trust Badges
- **Background:** Gradient (Sky Blue → Navy)
- **Icon:** Shield with checkmark
- **Text:** "Trusted" or "Secure"
- **Color:** White text
- **Usage:** Security, verified seller indicators

#### Community Badges
- **Background:** Light Cream
- **Border:** Sky Blue (2px)
- **Icon:** Users/community icon
- **Text:** "Community Benefit" or similar
- **Color:** Navy text

### Button Styles

#### Primary Button (CTA)
- **Background:** Bright Yellow (#D8B000)
- **Text:** Navy, Semibold
- **Padding:** 12-16px horizontal, 10-12px vertical
- **Border Radius:** 6px
- **Hover State:** Slightly darker yellow, slight shadow

#### Secondary Button
- **Background:** Transparent
- **Border:** 2px Sky Blue
- **Text:** Sky Blue, Semibold
- **Padding:** 10-14px horizontal, 8-10px vertical
- **Hover State:** Light blue background

#### Tertiary Button (Ghost)
- **Background:** Transparent
- **Text:** Navy, Medium
- **Hover State:** Light gray background

### Toast Notifications

#### Success Toast
- **Background Gradient:** Sky Blue → Light Cyan
- **Icon Color:** White
- **Text Color:** White
- **Border:** Subtle blue border (optional)

#### Error Toast
- **Background Gradient:** Red → Dark Red
- **Icon Color:** White
- **Text Color:** White
- **Border:** Subtle red border (optional)

#### Warning Toast
- **Background Gradient:** Yellow → Orange
- **Icon Color:** White
- **Text Color:** White or Navy
- **Border:** Subtle yellow border (optional)

#### Info Toast
- **Background Gradient:** Sky Blue → Light Blue
- **Icon Color:** White
- **Text Color:** White
- **Border:** Subtle blue border (optional)

### Icons & Illustrations

- **Style:** Line-based, minimal, consistent stroke width (2px)
- **Color:** Use primary colors, maintain consistency
- **Size:** Provide in 24x24, 32x32, 48x48 px
- **Format:** SVG for scalability

**Common Icons:**
- Shopping Cart (purchases)
- Heart (wishlist/favorite)
- Shield (security/trust)
- Users/Groups (community)
- Leaf (organic/eco-friendly)
- Discount percent (promotions)

## Application Guidelines

### Website & Web Applications

**Header:**
- Display horizontal banner logo (logo-banner-xl.png)
- Minimum height: 60px on mobile, 80px on desktop
- Transparent background required
- Responsive scaling with text navigation

**Footer:**
- Display shield logo (logo-shield-2x.png) centered above copyright
- 120-140px height
- 30-40px top/bottom padding
- Transparent background
- Followed by copyright text centered

**Landing Pages:**
- Use shield logo 256x256 in hero section if needed
- Use banner logo for main header
- Maintain 20% clear space around logos
- Ensure contrast against gradient backgrounds

### Social Media & Marketing

**Facebook/LinkedIn Banner:**
- Dimensions: 820x312px
- Place logo left or center
- Use brand colors for background
- Include tagline

**Instagram Story:**
- Dimensions: 1080x1920px
- Logo size: 256x256px minimum
- Top or center placement
- Ensure text contrast

**Twitter/X Post:**
- Logo size: 128x128px minimum
- Use in top-left or with product images
- Ensure 20% clear space

**Email Marketing:**
- Header: Use banner logo 300-600px wide
- Footer: Use shield logo 128px
- Maintain transparent backgrounds where possible
- Ensure readable on light backgrounds

### Print Materials (for future expansion)

**Business Cards:**
- Logo size: 0.5" x 0.5" minimum
- Placement: Top-left or bottom-right
- Background: White or light cream

**Brochures:**
- Cover: Use banner logo prominently (2-3" width)
- Interior: Shield logo 1" x 1" for section dividers
- Ensure print resolution: 300 DPI minimum

**Packaging:**
- Front: Shield logo 2" x 2" centered
- Back: Banner logo 3-4" width
- Use CMYK color mode for print

## Technical Specifications

### File Formats & Exports

**Logo Files Provided:**
- `favicon.ico` - 96x96px (Browser favicon)
- `favicon-16x16.png` - 16x16px
- `favicon-32x32.png` - 32x32px
- `favicon-48x48.png` - 48x48px
- `favicon-64x64.png` - 64x64px
- `apple-touch-icon.png` - 180x180px (iOS)
- `logo-shield.png` - 128x128px (Standard)
- `logo-shield-2x.png` - 512x512px (Large)
- `logo-banner-hd.png` - 600px width (HD banner)
- `logo-banner-xl.png` - 2800px width (Extra Large banner)

**Export Requirements:**
- All files: PNG format with transparency
- All files: sRGB color space
- Minimum resolution: 72 DPI (web), 300 DPI (print)
- Format for Canva: PNG with transparency

### Accessibility

- Ensure sufficient contrast ratio (WCAG AA minimum 4.5:1 for text)
- Alt text for all images: "Namecheap logo" or "Namecheap - Organic Beauty & Health Products"
- Avoid conveying information through color alone
- Use semantic HTML and ARIA labels

## Do's & Don'ts Summary

### ✅ DO:
- Use transparent backgrounds always
- Maintain aspect ratios when scaling
- Use official brand colors from palette
- Follow typography hierarchy
- Ensure minimum clear space (20%) around logos
- Use high-quality product images
- Maintain consistent voice and tone
- Test across all devices/platforms
- Update materials quarterly or as needed

### ❌ DON'T:
- Use logo on white or opaque backgrounds
- Alter colors without approval
- Use outdated or low-resolution files
- Mix fonts outside specified hierarchy
- Place logos closer than minimum clear space
- Use low-quality or heavily filtered product images
- Deviate from brand voice in copy
- Create new logo variations without design approval
- Use compressed or pixelated files
